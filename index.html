About

Tanushri is a B.Tech Information Technology undergraduate at Amity University, holding a 9.34 CGPA through her fifth semester while building hands-on frontend projects alongside her coursework. She pairs academic rigor with a builder's instinct — turning what she learns about interfaces, layout systems and interaction design into working websites and applications rather than leaving it in the textbook.

That same discipline was forged well before college, in the National Cadet Corps, where she rose through senior leadership roles and represented the MP & CG Directorate at the All India Thal Sainik Camp, returning with a Silver Medal. Competitive athletics sharpened the same instincts for pressure and pace. Her technical curiosity now stretches into national-level hackathons and term-paper research into tactical communication systems and robotics in military operations — a throughline from disciplined execution to applied technology.
